Template Purpose
This template instructs the LLM to generate high-fidelity image prompts that simulate realistic environmental lighting conditions for e-commerce product photography, ensuring products appear attractive, natural, and commercially viable.

FIXED FACTORS (Universal Rules - Apply to ALL Products)
A. Lighting Physics & Realism
Light behavior must follow real-world physics: Direction, intensity, falloff, color temperature, shadows, highlights, and reflections must be coherent and realistic

No artificial or lifeless appearance: Images must have depth, atmosphere, and dimensional quality

Appropriate shadow rendering: Shadows must match the lighting type (soft diffused for daylight, sharp for studio, colored for neon)

Specular highlights: Glossy/reflective surfaces must show appropriate catchlights and reflections consistent with the light source

Color temperature accuracy: Must specify Kelvin values or descriptive temperature (warm 3000K, neutral daylight 5600K, cool 6500K+)

B. Background & Staging Logic
White background input: If the reference image has a plain white/neutral background, generate an appropriate environmental context that:

Matches the product category and use case

Creates lifestyle appeal without overwhelming the product

Maintains product as the clear focal point

Ensures the staged environment is logical and realistic (e.g., furniture in interior spaces, kitchenware in kitchens, electronics on desks)

Raw/phone-captured input: If the reference image is a casual phone photo with cluttered or inconsistent lighting:

Clean and recontextualize the product in a professional environment

Apply the requested lighting type with proper staging

Remove distracting elements while preserving product authenticity

Environmental coherence: Background elements, props, and surfaces must harmonize with the lighting type (e.g., brick walls with warm daylight, minimalist surfaces for studio, urban elements for neon)

**Neon/RGB specific staging rules:**
- Background must be ABSTRACT and GEOMETRIC, never realistic or recognizable scenes
- Use clean surfaces, angular platforms, geometric shapes, or tech-inspired minimal forms [web:68][web:71]
- Neon tube lights should illuminate the scene but NOT be visible as background objects
- Light sources should be off-camera or highly abstracted (e.g., colored gradient washes, geometric light patterns)
- Avoid: cityscapes, street scenes, buildings, windows, storefronts, atmospheric smoke/steam that obscures product detail
- Goal: Create a clean, social-media-ready aesthetic that highlights the product with vibrant lighting while maintaining e-commerce clarity [web:70]

C. E-commerce Standards
Product prominence: Product must remain the primary subject; lighting should enhance, not distract

Color accuracy: Product colors must be true-to-life under the specified lighting condition

Detail preservation: Textures, materials, and fine details must be clearly visible

Professional composition: Follow rule of thirds, appropriate depth of field, and balanced framing

High resolution intent: Describe sharp focus, crisp details, and professional-grade clarity

D. Lighting Type Specifications
DAYLIGHT:

Natural sunlight characteristics (5500-6500K)

Specify time of day if relevant (golden hour 3000-4000K warm glow, midday 5500K+ bright and neutral, overcast 6000-7000K soft diffused)

Include directional cues (window light, outdoor shade, direct sun vs diffused)

Natural shadows with soft edges (unless harsh midday specified)

STUDIO:

Controlled artificial lighting (5000-5600K neutral white)

Specify setup: softbox, key light + fill light, rim/backlight if applicable

Minimal to no shadows (unless intentional for drama)

Clean, professional, evenly lit appearance

Background typically seamless or minimal

INDOOR WARM:

Warm artificial lighting (2700-3200K)

Cozy, intimate atmosphere

Typical sources: incandescent bulbs, lamps, warm LED, firelight ambiance

Soft gradients from light to shadow

Contextual interior environment (living room, bedroom, café, etc.)

NEON/RGB:
- Vibrant colored light sources (specify colors: cyan, magenta, purple, pink, blue, orange, etc.)
- Modern, tech-forward, or futuristic aesthetic
- Colored reflections and highlights on product surfaces
- BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT: Abstract, geometric, or minimalist designs that complement the product
  * Preferred backgrounds: Geometric shapes, tech patterns, gradient surfaces, angular platforms, abstract forms, clean architectural elements
  * AVOID: Realistic cityscapes, urban street scenes, buildings, recognizable environments, tube lights as background elements, atmospheric haze obscuring product
- Background should contrast or harmonize with product color while remaining non-distracting
- Dark or neutral base tones (black, dark grey, navy) with neon accent lighting
- Can include multiple colored light sources from different angles
- Product must remain in sharp focus; background can have subtle depth blur if needed

SUNSET/GOLDEN HOUR:

Warm directional light (2500-3500K orange/golden tones)

Long soft shadows

Atmospheric glow and haze

Rim lighting effect on edges

Rich warm color palette

VARIABLE FACTORS (Dynamic - Adapt to Product & User Requirements)
A. Product-Specific Adaptations
Scale reference (for large items like furniture, beds, appliances):

Include contextual scale indicators (human figure in grayscale for beds, room context for furniture, hand interaction for small items)

Material considerations:

Reflective/glossy products: Emphasize controlled reflections and catchlights

Matte products: Focus on form and texture through lighting gradients

Transparent products: Show light transmission and refraction

Fabric/textiles: Highlight texture, weave, and how light interacts with fibers

Functional context:

Show products in use scenarios appropriate to their category

For technical products: May require cleaner, more neutral presentations

For lifestyle products: Richer environmental storytelling

B. User-Specified Parameters
Integrate these user requirements into the prompt:

Lighting type (required): daylight/studio/indoor warm/neon/sunset/other

Intensity (if specified): Bright, moderate, soft, dramatic, etc.

Direction (if specified): Front-lit, side-lit (left/right), backlit, top-down, bottom-up

Additional mood/atmosphere (if specified): Moody, airy, dramatic, clean, cozy, vibrant, etc.

NEGATIVE PROMPT FRAMEWORK ⚠️ [CRITICAL ADDITION]
Purpose: Prevent common AI artifacts, hallucinations, and quality degradation that compromise e-commerce viability.

TESTING & ITERATION PROTOCOL
Phase 1: Baseline Test

Generate image with positive prompt only

Identify top 3-5 defects or artifacts

Document specific failure types

Phase 2: Core Negative Implementation

Add universal negative set (Categories 1, 2, 6)

Test with same product/lighting scenario

Evaluate artifact reduction

Phase 3: Refinement

If specific artifact persists (e.g., text in neon scenarios), add 5-10 variations of that negative term
​

For recurring issues, strengthen that negative category

Balance: Ensure negatives don't over-constrain creative output

Phase 4: Documentation

Save working positive + negative pairs as templates for similar products

Build a library of proven prompt combinations by product category

Note which negative terms are most effective for specific lighting types

CRITICAL NOTES
⚠️ For NEON/RGB lighting operations: Text artifacts are 3x more likely. Always apply maximum text prevention measures.

​⚠️ **For NEON/RGB backgrounds:** The most common failure is realistic urban/cityscape backgrounds that distract from the product. ALWAYS specify abstract geometric, minimalist, or tech-inspired backgrounds. Neon lighting should create atmosphere through COLOR and LIGHT QUALITY, not through literal environmental storytelling [web:68][web:70][web:71]. The background must remain clean and e-commerce/social-media appropriate.

⚠️ For products with actual text/labels (books, packaging, labeled electronics): Do NOT use text-removal negatives. Instead, use: "accurate product text reproduction, clear readable product labels as shown in reference image"

⚠️ Balance is key: Negative prompts should prevent unwanted artifacts WITHOUT restricting legitimate creative elements appropriate to the scene.
