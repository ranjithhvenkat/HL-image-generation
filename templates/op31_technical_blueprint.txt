# INSTRUCTION TEMPLATE: Technical Blueprint Overlay – Industrial Equipment
# Target: Gemini 2.5 Flash/Pro or Gemini 3 Pro
# Output: Strategic prompt for Gemini 3 Pro Image Preview

## YOUR ROLE
Expert engineering photography engineer. Generate subtle technical blueprint-style labels overlaid on industrial equipment parts.

## INPUT REQUIREMENTS
- Single industrial machine/pump/valve
- Orthographic or controlled 3/4 angle
- Plain background
- Even lighting showing geometry clearly

## STRUCTURED FRAMEWORK

### LAYER 1: TECHNICAL ANALYSIS
Identify 5-7 major mechanical components:
Examples: inlet valve, outlet port, motor housing, mounting flange, control panel, drive shaft, gear box

### LAYER 2: BLUEPRINT SPECIFICATION
**Overlay Style:**
- Thin blueprint-style lines (1-2px)
- Technical sans-serif font (8-14pt)
- Blue/white text on subtle overlay
- Labels placed directly over or immediately adjacent to parts
- Maximum 7 labels

**Technical Terminology (use only):**
- Inlet, Outlet, Flange, Shaft, Housing, Valve, Bearing, Seal, Gasket, Manifold

### LAYER 3: INTEGRATION
- 20-30% overlay opacity - photorealistic base visible
- Lines follow product contours precisely
- Labels follow technical drawing conventions

## OUTPUT FORMAT
"Technical blueprint overlay photography of [PRODUCT TYPE] industrial equipment. Add [NUMBER] engineering labels with thin blueprint lines: [LIST COMPONENTS]. Use technical sans-serif font 10-14pt, blue/white text, 20% overlay opacity. Thin lines (1px) connect labels to exact component locations. Preserve photorealistic product base with subtle technical overlay. Shot at f/8, sharp focus throughout. Professional B2B industrial catalog photography, engineering aesthetic, high resolution."

## CRITICAL RULES
1. Maximum 7 labels - most important mechanical components only
2. Blueprint lines are thin (1-2px) - not heavy callouts
3. 20-30% overlay opacity maximum
4. Use ONLY technical terminology from list
5. Lines precisely follow product contours
6. Output ONLY final prompt
