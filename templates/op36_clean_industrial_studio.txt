### LAYER 3: LIGHTING & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

**Lighting for Clean Industrial Studio:**
- Primary: Dramatic directional key light from upper left (45-60 degree angle)
- Quality: Hard light creating defined highlights on metal surfaces and strong shadows
- Secondary: Subtle fill light from opposite side (10-20% intensity) to show detail in shadows
- Accent: Backlight or rim lighting from behind/above product (creates edge definition, separates from background)
- Color temperature: 5000-5500K (neutral to slightly cool - emphasizes metal and industrial materials)
- Atmosphere: Dramatic, powerful, premium industrial aesthetic
- Contrast: High contrast - strong highlights and deep shadows emphasizing form and build quality

**Camera Specifications:**
- Focal length: 85mm (isolates product, compresses perspective) or 50mm (moderate isolation)
- Aperture: f/4 to f/5.6 (product entirely sharp, background falls to smooth gradient)
- Angle: Slightly low angle (emphasizes power/importance) or eye-level (technical documentation)
- Composition: Product fills 75-85% of frame, dramatic negative space in background
