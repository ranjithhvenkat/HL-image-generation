PURPOSE
Generate a detailed image generation prompt for creating a multi-product composite layout (kit/set/bundle view) that meets e-commerce quality standards and maximizes conversion potential.

PART A: FIXED FACTORS (Universal rules applicable to all multi-product composites)
1. Composition Architecture
Visual Hierarchy: Identify and position the hero/primary product at rule-of-thirds intersection points (preferably lower-right for Z-pattern reading flow)
​

Alignment Precision: All products must be aligned symmetrically or follow deliberate geometric patterns (grid, radial, linear cluster); avoid accidental misalignment
​

Negative Space Management: Maintain 15-25% negative space around the composition to prevent visual overcrowding; products should not touch frame edges

Frame Fill Ratio: Products collectively should occupy 70-80% of the frame while maintaining breathing room between individual items
​

2. Spatial Organization Rules
Inter-product Spacing: Maintain consistent spacing between items (proportional to smallest product dimension); spacing should be uniform unless asymmetry is deliberate and patterned
​

Depth & Layering (if applicable): For flat-lay compositions, create subtle depth through strategic overlapping (max 20% overlap) or micro-height variations; for elevated views, use front-to-back scaling
​

Scale Consistency: All products must appear at their true relative sizes unless the user specifies dimensional emphasis

3. Visual Quality Standards
Lighting Uniformity: Soft, diffused lighting with minimal harsh shadows; maintain consistent light temperature (5500-6500K for neutral) across all products
​

Focus Distribution: All products in the composition must be in sharp focus (simulated deep depth of field, f/8-f/11 equivalent)
​

Color Accuracy: Maintain true-to-life product colors; avoid color casts; background/props must not compete chromatically with products
​

Shadow Behavior: Implement subtle, consistent drop shadows (15-25% opacity) in the same direction for all items to anchor them spatially
​

4. Background Treatment
Default Standard: Clean, pure white background (RGB 255,255,255) unless user specifies otherwise

Alternative Contexts: If lifestyle/contextual background is required, ensure it remains visually recessive (desaturated, soft-focus) and never competes with product clarity
​

Surface Texture: If surface is visible, apply subtle texture (wood grain, marble, fabric weave) that enhances premium perception without distraction
​

5. Technical Output Specifications
Resolution Directive: Ultra-high resolution, photo-realistic rendering suitable for e-commerce zoom functionality
​

Aspect Ratio Consideration: Compose for 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait) ratios unless user specifies; ensure no critical elements are positioned in potential crop zones
​

File Cleanliness: No visible watermarks, text overlays, price tags, or graphical embellishments on products
​

PART D: EDGE CASE HANDLING
Highly Varied Product Sizes (e.g., large case + small accessories):

Position largest item as anchor in background/center

Arrange smaller items in foreground or clustered grouping

Maintain visual weight balance through strategic positioning

Monochrome Product Sets (all products same color):

Increase reliance on lighting contrast and shadow definition

Consider subtle background tint (complementary color) to prevent flatness

Emphasize texture and form through directional lighting

Reflective/Glass Products:

Specify "controlled reflections with gradient lighting to minimize glare"

Position matte products adjacent to glass items for visual contrast

Vertical vs. Horizontal Dominant Products:

For bottles/tall items: Use linear horizontal arrangement or radial pattern

For flat items: Stack with slight offset or grid arrangement

Food/Perishable Products:

Emphasize freshness through color saturation and highlight placement

Include minimal contextual elements (cutting board texture, linen fabric)
